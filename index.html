🏛️ 7 Wonders - Rules & Strategy Reference

Core Objective & Flow

You lead one of the seven great cities of the Ancient World. Over 3 distinct Ages (Age I, Age II, Age III), you draft cards from rotating hands to develop resources, civilian prestige, commerce, military strength, scientific inventions, and construct your Wonder.

🃏 Draft & Simultaneous Play

Each turn, choose 1 card from your 7-card hand. All players reveal simultaneously. Then remaining cards pass to your neighbor (Age I: Left, Age II: Right, Age III: Left). After 6 turns, the 7th card is discarded.

🏛️ 3 Ways to Play a Card

1. Build Structure: Pay resource/coin cost and place in your city.
2. Build Wonder Stage: Pay wonder stage cost, tuck card face-down under your wonder board.
3. Discard for 3 Coins: Gain 3 coins immediately from the bank.

🤝 Neighbor Trading

You can buy raw materials (wood, stone, ore, clay) and manufactured goods (glass, loom, papyrus) from your immediate Left or Right neighbor's fixed production. Base cost is 2 coins per resource, reduced to 1 coin with commercial trading posts!

📜 Scientific Mastery Formula

Science cards feature 3 symbols: 🧭 Compass, ⚙️ Gear, 📜 Tablet.
• Each symbol type: $count^2$ points (e.g. 3 gears = 9 pts).
• Complete sets of all 3 symbols: +7 bonus points per set!
Example: 3 gears, 2 tablets, 2 compasses = (9 + 4 + 4) + 14 = 31 VP!

⚔️ Military Conflicts

At the end of each Age, compare your shields against your immediate Left and Right neighbor.
• Age I: Win = +1 VP, Loss = -1 VP.
• Age II: Win = +3 VP, Loss = -1 VP.
• Age III: Win = +5 VP, Loss = -1 VP.

🔗 Chaining Trees

Many cards feature building chains. If you constructed the predecessor building in an earlier age (e.g. Baths), you can build the successor (Aqueduct) completely for FREE without paying its resource cost!
